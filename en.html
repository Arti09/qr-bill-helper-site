What it does

Finds QR-bills

In images on web pages and in webmail (e.g. Gmail, Outlook on the web) — with a green label right at the code.

Reads PDFs too

In Chrome's PDF viewer and in previews, the popup scans the screen by itself.

Copies what you need

Each field on its own, or "Copy all" as text, a table row, or IBAN;amount;reference.

Checks before you pay

IBAN check digits, QR-IBAN and QR reference, QRR and SCOR check digits, amount and currency.

Prevents paying twice

Bills you copied before show "copied on …".

Stays on your computer

No server, no analytics, no ads. Bill data never leaves your computer.

FAQ

Which banks does it work with?

All of them: the extension copies the payment details, you paste them into your bank's e-banking. No bank login needed.

Is my data safe?

QR codes are read locally in your browser. Only the Pro license check contacts ExtensionPay; bill data is never sent. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the extension's popup, click "Manage subscription" at the bottom. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes: within 14 days of the first payment we refund the full amount on request by e-mail, no reason needed.
